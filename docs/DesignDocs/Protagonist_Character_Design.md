## 2. Overall Silhouette

### 2.1 Design Principles

**Three visual anchors:**
1. **Asymmetric coat cut** — right side longer, left side shorter; the coat's uneven hem creates a distinctive walking silhouette
2. **Scarred face** — left cheek diagonal scar as the primary facial identifier; bare head with nothing above the hairline
3. **Faded-red scarf** — the only saturated color pop, wrapped around the neck, draws the eye to the face

### 2.2 Design Rationale for Clean-Clothed Wastelander

The protagonist is not a ragged beggar — he is a survivor who **moves between worlds**: the wasteland and mage society. His clothing must be:

| Principle | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| **Clean, intact** | Can pass as a commoner or low-ranking servant in mage cities. Tears and holes would draw unwanted attention. |
| **Wasteland materials** | Coarse linen, heavy cotton, weathered leather — materials mages wouldn't use, marking his outsider origin to the player |
| **No armor** | Armor signals combat intent. A drifter in cloth is underestimated — which is the point. |
| **No visible weapons** | Weapons are confiscated at mage city gates. He carries nothing that marks him as a threat. |
| **Bare head** | Necessary for the mask equipment slot. Masks sit directly on the face with no headband interference. |

## 3. Head & Face (Bare — No Headwear)

> **Important:** The head is completely bare above the hairline. No headband, no hood, no hat, no hair ornaments. This keeps the face fully visible as the primary recognition point and leaves the head clear for the mask equipment slot (see §5.6).

### 3.1 Hair

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Length** | Medium-long, just covers ears, slightly longer at the nape |
| **Texture** | Rough, slightly tangled — no water for grooming in the Hole, but kept reasonably clean for blending in |
| **Color** | Dark brown to near-black, with a few streaks bleached grey-white by cave mineral dust |
| **Style** | Naturally falls forward, fringe half-covers brows. No ties, no bands — loose and unbound. In combat/wind, hair blows back to reveal the full face. |

### 3.2 Facial Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Face shape** | Lean angular face, prominent cheekbones, strong jawline |
| **Skin tone** | Pale from cave life + sun-exposure tan (wheat undertone) |
| **Eyebrows** | Thick, dark, prominent brow ridge; right brow has an old scar bisecting the tail |
| **Eyes** | Deep-set dark brown, hawk-like focused gaze — the stare of a lifelong survivor |
| **Nose** | Straight bridge with a slight crook mid-bridge (old injury) |
| **Lips** | Thin, corners slightly downturned — perpetual alertness, but can soften when blending into a crowd |
| **Scars** | Diagonal slash across left cheek (zygomatic to jaw) — **the primary facial identifier**; right brow scar; shallow abrasion marks on chin |

### 3.3 Mouth & Jaw (Fully Exposed)

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Mouth** | Thin lips, corners slightly downturned at rest — perpetual alertness. Upper lip has a faint vertical scar near the left corner |
| **Jaw** | Strong angular jawline, masseter muscle visible when clenched |
| **Chin** | Slightly cleft, with a shallow diagonal abrasion mark |
| **Teeth** | Slightly yellowed (no dental care in the Hole), but intact. Right canine is noticeably chipped |
| **Facial hair** | Perpetual light stubble — shaving is a luxury in the Hole. Slightly denser around the jawline and chin |

### 3.4 No Headwear — Design Intent

- The bare head makes the **face the undisputed focal point** — scars, eyes, and expressions carry all visual weight
- Masks (see §5.4) occupy the face directly, attached behind the ears and at the back of the head
- No headband means no clipping issues with any mask type
- Hair alone frames the face — when masks are equipped, hair + mask create the facial silhouette

## 5. Clothing (Layered Breakdown — No Armor, No Weapons)

Clothing uses a layered design approach for clean 3D modeling separation and future equipment-swap support. **All pieces are fabric or soft leather — no hard armor components, no weapon mounts, no tool attachments.**

### 5.1 Base Layer

| Piece | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Undershirt** | Coarse linen sleeveless tunic, originally off-white, now a muted grey-beige from mineral dust and repeated washing. Clean, no holes. |
| **Undergarment** | Cotton long underdrawers, dark grey. Simple, functional. |
| **Arm wraps** | Both forearms wrapped in burlap-linen strips (wrist to elbow) — practical hand protection, absorbs sweat. Wraps are even and neat, not ragged. |
| **Leg wraps** | Calf bindings in cloth strips (ankle to below knee) — protection during climbing, keeps trousers clear of debris. Tightly wound, clean. |

### 5.2 Mid Layer

| Piece | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Tunic** | Thick cotton-linen blend long-sleeve tunic, dark grey-brown. Simple cut, no embellishments. Hem reaches mid-thigh, slit at sides for mobility. Collar sits open at the neck — the scarf fills the gap. |
| **Trousers** | Heavy canvas work trousers, dark grey. Straight-leg cut, slightly loose for movement. No patches, no holes — well-maintained. |
| **Belt** | Single wide leather belt, dark brown. Plain iron buckle, no ornament. No tool loops, no weapon mounts — purely functional. |

### 5.3 Outer Layer — The Coat

The coat is the **primary visual element** — replacing the old armor-based silhouette with a fabric-based one.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Type** | Long asymmetrical coat / duster, mid-calf length |
| **Material** | Heavy waxed canvas, weather-resistant. Dark grey-brown with subtle wasteland dust tones. |
| **Cut** | **Asymmetric hem** — right side hangs to mid-calf, left side ends at knee. This is the #1 silhouette identifier from all angles. |
| **Collar** | Stand-up mandarin-style collar, left open at the front. Frames the scarf and neck. |
| **Shoulders** | Natural shoulder line, no padding. Fabric drapes cleanly — no pauldrons, no reinforcement. |
| **Sleeves** | Full-length, straight cut, slightly wide at the cuff. Cuffs have a single roll-up when forearms are wrapped. |
| **Closure** | Off-center front closure (right-to-left), secured with three wooden toggles and cord loops — distinctly non-mage, wasteland craftsmanship. |
| **Back** | Single rear vent from waist to hem for movement. A subtle horizontal seam at shoulder-blade level — the only structural seam visible. |
| **Texture** | Smooth but matte. The waxed canvas has a slight sheen in direct light, distinguishing it from the matte tunic underneath. Subtle dust accumulation in seam creases. |
| **Wear** | Edges are softly worn (not torn), hem has a slight uneven fade from dragging through cave dust. No holes. No patches. |

**Coat design language:**
- The asymmetric cut echoes the wasteland origin without looking barbaric
- Wooden toggles instead of buttons — available in the Hole, inconspicuous in the mage world
- The coat can be worn open (exploration) or closed (infiltration/stealth) — two distinct silhouettes
- From behind, the coat's long right panel creates a subtle swaying motion when walking

### 5.4 The Scarf

- A long coarse-cloth scarf wrapped around the **neck** (not the head), one end draping over the chest, the other thrown behind the back.
- Color: **faded dark red** — the only higher-saturation element on the entire character
- Originally bright red — possibly a symbol of identity, now unreadable
- Frayed edges from wear, but the fabric body is intact
- Sits above the coat collar, creating a color transition from the bare face → red scarf → muted coat

### 5.5 Mask (Equipment Slot — Head)

> Masks are the **only head equipment**. The bare-head default ensures masks sit cleanly on the face with no interference from headbands, hoods, or hair ornaments.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Equipment slot** | Mask — dedicated slot. Attaches behind the ears and at the back of the head. |
| **Default state** | No mask — face fully visible, bare head |
| **Acquisition** | First mask obtained from the tutorial mage fight (Prologue); better masks drop from bosses and rare enemies |
| **Gameplay effect** | Each mask type grants specific bonuses (e.g., wider block window, smoke gain +10%, skill cost reduction) — see GDD §6.3.2 |

**Mask design template** (individual mask variants follow this base structure):

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Type** | Half-mask, covering from nose bridge down to chin. Upper face (eyes, brows, forehead) remains exposed. |
| **Material** | Hardened leather + metal framework + embedded shards (material upgrades with rarity) |
| **Shape** | Contoured arc hugging the face; a vertical metal ridge runs from nose bridge to chin center |
| **Attachment** | Secured by thin straps behind the ears and at the back of the head — no interference with hair |
| **Texture** | Battle-worn — smoke-scorch marks and cracks varying by mask tier |

**Planned mask tiers (aesthetic direction only — stats TBD in GDD):**

| Tier | Visual Appearance | Example Source |
|------|------------------|----------------|
| **Crude (starter)** | Coarse cloth wrap, no metal. Uneven edges, stained | Tutorial mage |
| **Common** | Hardened leather, basic metal ridge. Few scorch marks | Apprentice mages |
| **Rare** | Leather + thin iron, ceramic shard inlays, dark red/blue accents | Adept mages, mini-bosses |
| **Legendary** | Multi-material (demon leather + obsidian shards + gold wire inlay), faint glow | Bosses |
| **Demon** | Organic-looking chitin + pulsing veins of light, jagged asymmetric shape | Demon enemies, Satan |

### 5.6 Footwear

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Type** | Mid-calf leather boots, soft toe (no metal cap) |
| **Material** | Worn dark brown leather, multiple pieces stitched together |
| **Sole** | Thick layered leather sole, stitched not nailed — quiet on stone floors |
| **Closure** | Side buckle at the ankle, no laces |
| **Condition** | Well-maintained but visibly old — creases at the ankle flex point, sole edges softened from wear |

### 5.7 What's NOT on the Character

| Removed (v1.1) | Reason |
|----------------|--------|
| Right pauldron (armor) | No armor — coat drapes naturally over shoulders |
| Left shoulder guard (armor) | No armor |
| Chest plate (armor) | No armor |
| Gorget / neck guard (armor) | No armor — scarf provides visual interest at neck |
| Vambrace / bracer (armor) | No armor — arm wraps only |
| Knee guards (armor) | No armor |
| Headband | Bare head for mask slot; no head decorations |
| Short cape | Replaced by the asymmetrical coat |
| Dagger and sheath | No weapons |
| Smoke collection vials | No visible tools |
| Tool pouch | No visible equipment |
| Spare gear loops | No visible equipment |
| Rope coil | No visible equipment |
| Thigh strap pouch | No visible equipment |

### 6.3 Palette Principles

- Overall low saturation, warm grey-brown dominant — evokes the Hole's oppressive atmosphere while looking neat enough to pass among mages
- The faded-red scarf is the **sole higher-saturation accent** — draws visual focus to the face
- No metal tones beyond the belt buckle — reinforces "no armor, no weapons"
- Waxed canvas has a subtle cool undertone (`#5A4D42`) that distinguishes it from the warmer tunic (`#8A8578`) — creates visual depth without armor's hard contrasts
- All fabrics are within 2–3 value steps of each other — harmonious, understated, blends into crowds

### 7.4 45° View (for Combat UI & Character Select)

This is the most frequently seen angle in-game (character positioned to one side in combat):

- The coat's asymmetric hem creates a dynamic diagonal at 45° — the #1 silhouette identifier
- The diagonal left cheek scar catches rim light at 45° — a key facial recognition feature
- The scarf's faded red creates a color transition from bare face → red → coat grey-brown at 45°
- The wooden toggle closure becomes visible at this angle — subtle wasteland detail
- The coat's longer right panel trails behind — adds motion to combat stance
- No armor glints, no weapon silhouettes — the character reads as unarmed, which makes combat animations more surprising

## 8. Expression Reference

Close-up face shots occur during Clash events (same-color collision → real-time block/dodge). Expressions must read clearly on the **bare face with no headwear**.

| Expression | Trigger | Key Features |
|------------|---------|--------------|
| **Default (Alert)** | Exploration / idle | Slightly furrowed brows, sharp focused eyes, mouth corners slightly down, jaw tense |
| **Blending In** | Infiltration / mage city areas | Face relaxed, eyes soft but watchful, neutral mouth — the expression of someone who belongs here |
| **Anger / Combat** | Attacking / charging | Brows pressed down over eyes, nostrils flared, teeth clenched, neck muscles tensed |
| **Pain / Hit** | Taking damage / failed block | Eyes closed (one or both), brows tight, mouth open, head recoiling back |
| **Focus / Blocking** | Clash block moment | Eyes wide open, pupils contracted, lips pressed tight, sweat-matted fringe on forehead |
| **Exhaustion** | Low HP / smoke depleted | Eyes half-lidded, forehead sweat, blood at corner of mouth, mouth open breathing, head slightly lowered |
| **Relief / Relaxed** | Dialogue (safe zone) | Brows relaxed, eyes soft (but still a trace of alertness), slight upward mouth corner |
| **Determination** | Pre-boss / key decision | Eyes fixed forward unwavering, jaw tight, lips pressed into a thin line, slight chin lift |

**Expression design principles:**
- With bare head and no mask by default, the **entire face** conveys emotion — eyes + brows + mouth + jaw work together
- When a mask is equipped (covering lower face), expression delivery shifts to **eyes and brows** as primary channels
- Muscle movement must be realistic — no exaggerated cartoon deformation
- Fine wrinkles and skin folds around the eyes enhance realism
- The diagonal cheek scar stretches and deforms with expression changes, adding dynamic facial detail
- Hair movement (falling forward, blowing back) adds secondary emotional cues — hair obscuring eyes = exhaustion/defeat

## 9. Appearance Progression

The protagonist's appearance evolves subtly through equipment upgrades acquired through smoke:

| Stage | Visual Changes |
|-------|---------------|
| **Prologue / Initial** | Basic clothing only. Simple cloth tunic, no coat. No scarf — acquired from an NPC during the prologue. Face bare (no mask). Boots are more worn, wraps are simpler. |
| **Cycle 1–2** | The coat is acquired — immediately transforms the silhouette. Scarf obtained. First crude mask may be obtained and optionally equipped. Arm wraps and leg wraps upgraded to better material. |
| **Cycle 3** | Coat material upgrades — better waxed canvas, subtle texture improvement. Tunic and trousers replaced with higher-quality versions (same cut, better fabric). Belt upgraded (same style, richer leather). Better mask tiers become available as drops. |
| **Cycle 4+ (Final)** | Coat gains subtle wasteland-world detailing — faint embroidered reinforcement at stress points (shoulders, elbows). Face may gain additional scars from accumulated battles. Legendary/Demon tier masks available. |

**Progression philosophy:**
- The coat is the primary progression-visible item — it changes material quality and subtle detailing, but the cut remains constant
- The silhouette never fundamentally changes — recognition must be instant at all stages
- Improvements are in fabric quality, not in adding armor or weapons — the character remains visibly unarmed throughout
- Mask tier is the most visible progression marker on the face

## 10. Female Variant

If a female protagonist is later unlocked, retain all design language with these adjustments:

- Height: ~170–175 cm
- Build: leaner but retaining functional muscle (climbing/survival muscle — not decorative)
- Face: same scar pattern (left cheek diagonal), slightly narrower jaw, thinner brows
- Hair: same loose medium-long style, or optional low ponytail (keeps hair clear, still bare head for masks)
- All clothing dimensions scaled proportionally; design language unchanged
- Body differences do not affect silhouette recognition — the asymmetric coat + scarred face + red scarf triangle remains intact

## 12. 3D Modeling Technical Notes

| Item | Recommendation |
|------|---------------|
| **Poly budget** | Protagonist: ~50K–80K triangles (LOD0); combat close-ups need high-poly face |
| **Bone count** | ~60–80 bones (including dynamic bones for coat panels/scarf/hair) |
| **Material IDs** | Skin (SSS), Fabric (coat), Fabric (tunic/trousers), Leather (belt/boots), Hair — each independent material |
| **Texture resolution** | Body 4K; Face 2K separate (expression detail requires it) |
| **Cloth physics** | Coat panels, scarf, hair → UE5 Chaos Cloth. Coat is the most important physics-driven element — the asymmetric hem sway is a key character identifier. |
| **LOD levels** | LOD0 (80K) → LOD1 (25K) → LOD2 (8K) — exploration mode typically uses LOD1 |
| **Key difference from v1.1** | No rigid armor pieces → all materials are soft-body cloth or leather. Simpler rigging (no pauldron bones, no weapon attachment points). |
